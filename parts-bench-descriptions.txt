LONG (about 1,000 characters)
======================================================================
Parts Bench is a free vehicle maintenance, garage and parts app for people who work on their own cars, trucks and SUVs, and for anyone who wants to stay on top of upkeep.

Enter a VIN and Parts Bench identifies the vehicle and checks it for safety recalls. Save vehicles to your Garage and the app builds a maintenance schedule for each one: oil and filter, tire rotation, air filters, wipers, and more. Log each service with the date, mileage, cost and notes, and Parts Bench shows what is overdue and what is coming up. Your mileage is estimated between updates so due dates stay accurate.

When something is due, tap through to Shop. Parts Bench searches live listings and links you to the right parts for your exact vehicle at eBay, Amazon and other stores.

Parts Bench is free to use. A Premium plan adds the full maintenance schedule, per-tire and per-axle tracking, intervals tuned to hybrids, EVs and all-wheel drive, email reminders, cost totals and a printable service history, and unlimited vehicles.

Some store links are affiliate links. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.
